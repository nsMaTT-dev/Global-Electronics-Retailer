# Conclusion

### Products & Customer Distribution

The company offers products across eight categories, including audio, cameras and camcorders, cell phones, computers, games and toys, home appliances, music and media, and TV and video.

The United States has the largest customer base among the countries represented in the dataset, followed by the **United Kingdom, Canada, Germany, and Australia**.

### Revenue Trends

Annual revenue increased substantially between 2016 and 2019, reaching approximately **$2.16 billion in 2019**. Revenue subsequently declined to approximately **$870 million in 2020 and $20 million in 2021**.

Monthly revenue also varies considerably across the calendar year.

### Data Limitation

The available data does not establish the causes of these changes. The sharp decline in 2021 should be interpreted cautiously because the period covered by the dataset may be incomplete.

### Order Trends

The annual number of distinct orders increased from **79 in 2016 to 418 in 2019**, before declining to 167 in 2020 and four in 2021.

When all available years are combined, January records the highest monthly order count, with **162 orders**, while December records the lowest, with **18 orders**.

### Key Observation

Order volume varies across months and years. However, the aggregated monthly view does not establish whether the same seasonal pattern occurs consistently in every year.

### Delivery Performance

The calculated average delivery time was **157 days in 2016, 121 in 2017, 125 in 2018, 119 in 2019, 97 in 2020, and four in 2021**.

However, these results require careful interpretation.

### Data Quality Limitation

The calculated delivery intervals are unusually long, with some records reaching **330 days**. Although the calculation measures the difference between order and delivery dates, the available analysis does not establish whether all recorded intervals accurately represent actual delivery times.

Missing delivery dates also mean that the averages represent only records with valid calculated intervals.

Therefore, these results should not be treated as a reliable measure of logistical efficiency without further validation of the source data.

### Average Order Value by Sales Channel

The dataset did not provide an identified field that reliably distinguishes online sales from in-store sales.

Consequently, the average order value could not be compared between these channels without making unsupported assumptions about the sales channel associated with each transaction.
